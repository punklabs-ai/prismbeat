# Visual catalogue

All visuals are authored on a shared 800×100 canonical canvas. PrismBeat maps that continuously across either the four-segment 800×100 Stream Deck + strip or the six-segment 1200×100 Stream Deck + XL strip. Colours move from cyan through blue and violet to pink/orange, while frequency layouts consistently place lows before highs unless a visual explicitly uses a vertical frequency axis.

## 1. Spectrum analyser

Forty low-left/high-right columns with twelve full-height blocks and white peak markers. Attack and release smoothing keep transients readable without making the display twitch.

## 2. Triggered oscilloscope

A real 32 ms waveform begins near a rising zero crossing. Five captured frames form coloured CRT-style persistence trails; no procedural wave is substituted for the audio.

## 3. Rectangular frequency tunnel

Fifteen four-corner history frames recede towards a central vanishing point. Bass affects tunnel size, mids bend its centre, treble changes line detail and beats push rings towards the viewer.

## 4. Audio warp starfield

Loudness controls star density and forward speed, treble lengthens trails, mids expand the field and beats create hyperspace bursts.

## 5. Spectrum plasma clouds

Twelve compressed spectrum zones drive cloud position, size, colour and opacity. Bass produces broad low-frequency masses, mids flow across the canvas and treble adds smaller bright structures.

## 6. Kaleidoscope petals

Three wheels map ordered bands across two counter-rotating petal rings. Band energy grows each petal and beat energy blooms the centres.

## 7. Elliptical spectrum halo

All forty bands radiate around a display-shaped elliptical core. Each band independently controls its ray length, thickness and brightness.

## 8. Neon spectrum terrain

Twelve real spectrum-history ridges create a perspective mesh. Low frequencies remain on the left, highs on the right, and newer frames approach the foreground.

## 9. Centre-origin spectrogram

Frequency rises from the bottom to the top while new time slices originate at the centre and move symmetrically outwards. Colour encodes intensity.

## 10. Frequency particle fountain

Three emitters launch frequency-tagged particles. Bass controls launch energy, mids widen the spray and treble creates bright streaks.

## 11. Stereo vectorscope

Four square CRT scopes draw left-channel amplitude on X and right-channel amplitude on Y. Mono material tends towards a diagonal; phase and stereo-width differences open into loops and more complex traces.

## 12. Frequency lightning storm

A full-width bolt uses spectrum levels and midrange motion for its path. Bass controls the main bolt's weight, treble bands create branches and beats trigger flashes.

## 13. Cymatic ripple pool

Five emitters divide the spectrum into eight-band regions. Their colour-coded rings expand, intersect and fade according to local energy and beat strength.

## 14. Frequency DNA helix

Two coloured strands twist across the full width. Bass changes their separation, mids alter twist density, individual bands shape local amplitude and treble illuminates the frequency-mapped rungs.

## 15. Pitch-class constellation

Twelve fixed stars represent C through B. Chromagram energy changes their size and brightness; the six strongest active pitch classes form a chord-driven connection network.

## 16. Frequency-driven digital rain

Forty possible rain columns map low-left to high-right. Band energy controls stream count, speed, trail length and weight. Each stream resets only after its complete glowing tail has passed below the display.

## 17. Synthwave outrun highway

A striped neon sunset sits behind a low-left/high-right frequency mountain ridge. Loudness and mids expand and brighten the sun, treble illuminates the stars and grid detail, while bass and beats strengthen the road edges and forward-moving perspective grid.

## 18. Neon city cruise

Thirty-two skyline buildings sample the ordered FFT bands, so bass lives on the left and treble on the right. Band energy raises individual towers, treble lights their windows and beats flash the horizon above a speed-sensitive perspective grid.

## 19. Miami night run

Palm silhouettes frame a striped coastal sunset. Five ocean traces combine the captured waveform with ordered spectrum energy; bass sways and grows the palms while treble sharpens the water and fronds.

## Shared behaviour

- Turning a dial assigned to visual selection in either direction wraps across all nineteen choices.
- Changing selection resets the visual phase and reveals centred indicator dots.
- Indicators automatically hide after five seconds.
- Silent states remain intentionally subdued instead of pretending to react.
- Status messaging overlays the canvas only when capture is starting, unavailable or waiting for Spotify audio.
